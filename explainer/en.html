A field-grounded argument · 2026

Rent the engine. Own the accumulation.

The durable value is not one model call, but the inspectable chain of decisions, corrections, and open questions that the next session can inherit. That chain can make pickup and handoff more direct. This paper does not claim it caused faster work.

03 · Two-time memory

Do not rewrite yesterday with today’s knowledge.

The record should show what governed at event time and what became known later. Otherwise the past looks cleaner—and less useful—than it was.

04 · Context hygiene

Read selectively. Brake when evidence thins.

No pickup begins by rereading everything. A maintained chain should expose what governs now, why it changed, and where uncertainty remains.

05 · Honest limits

Inspectable continuity is not proven acceleration.

The record supports a route through inherited work. Several stronger interpretations remain outside the evidence.

NO CAUSAL SPEED CLAIM

Task familiarity, editorial repetition, or a better brief could explain a quicker start.

NO LOSSLESS-HANDOFF CLAIM

One 9/9 transfer shows continuity of a bounded package across one interruption.

NO CONSCIOUSNESS CLAIM

Identity here means inspectable responsibility and commitments across sessions.

NO FULL BI-TEMPORAL SYSTEM

The practice distinguishes event time and knowledge time through provenance and supersession.

RECORDS CAN PRESERVE ERRORS

Ownership does not make inherited text true. Old entries still require review and correction.

MISSING PROVENANCE STAYS MISSING

Some entries cannot be verified because their source relationship was never retained.
